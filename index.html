Help

Use labelled bracket notation. This app will build the tree as you type and will attempt to close any brackets that you may be missing. Save the image to your computer by right-clicking on it and selecting "Save image as". For more information, including on how to draw movement lines, visit the wiki.

Feature requirements can be added by placing them inside curly braces right after a label (e.g. [DP{•N•} [NP ...]]). Each marker is rendered beneath the node as a bracketed item such as [•N•]; separate multiple markers with commas, semicolons, literal line breaks, or escaped \n sequences within the braces. Surround any label or lexical item with hyphens (e.g. -had-) to render it with a strikethrough.

Examples

[NP^ Alice]
[NP [N Alice] and [N Bob]]
[S[NP[N Alice]][VP[V is][NP[N'[N a student][PP^ of physics
[S [X_a Movement] [Y example <a>]]
